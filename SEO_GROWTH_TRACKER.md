## 7. Monthly SEO Review Workflow

1. **Check Coverage & Sitemaps (`Indexing > Pages` & `Sitemaps`)**:
   - Confirm `https://one-tool-hub-sooty.vercel.app/sitemap.xml` shows status **Success** with `23` discovered URLs once Step 7 is deployed.
   - Inspect any URLs marked *Crawled - currently not indexed* or *Discovered - currently not indexed*.
2. **Audit Striking-Distance Queries (`Performance > Search results`)**:
   - Filter queries with `Position > 7` and `Position < 20`.
   - Expand the corresponding tutorial (`/learn/[slug]`) or tool FAQ (`/tools/[slug]`) with a direct, accurate answer to the query.
3. **Improve CTR on High-Impression Pages**:
   - If a tool or tutorial has `> 500` impressions per month but `CTR < 2%`, review the `<title>` and `<meta name="description">` to ensure the primary benefit (browser-processed, no signup, instant download) is clear.
4. **Correlate with Consent-Gated GA4 Events (`G-Y6CKKYZDWM`)**:
   - Compare consented `tool_open`, `tool_process_success`, and `tool_download` counts in GA4 (`Reports > Engagement > Events`) to measure whether visitors arriving on `/learn/[slug]` tutorials click through to use the companion `/tools/[slug]` utilities.
