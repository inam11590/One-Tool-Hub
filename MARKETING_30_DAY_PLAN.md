# OneToolHub — 30-Day Organic Marketing & User Acquisition Plan

**Project**: OneToolHub (`https://one-tool-hub-sooty.vercel.app`)  
**Target Audience**: Students, Freelancers, YouTubers, Developers  
**Execution Context**: Solo developer with 20–40 minutes daily bandwidth  
**Core Growth Philosophy**: Value-first distribution, ethical transparency (no fake testimonials, no undisclosed self-promotion, zero spam), community problem-solving, and sustainable search visibility.

---

## 1. Operating Rules & Ethical Standards

1. **Full Transparency**: Always disclose project ownership (*"I built this free tool..."* or *"Full disclosure: I am the creator of OneToolHub"*).
2. **Never Spam**: Only share a link when it directly and completely answers an active question or discussion. If a post doesn't need a link, provide the solution in plain text and mention the tool only as an optional utility.
3. **Respect Rules**: Check subreddit rules, Discord channel policies, and LinkedIn group guidelines before posting.
4. **Zero Automation**: Never use bots, automated submission scripts, or mass scrapers.
5. **No Fake Social Proof**: Never fabricate user reviews, client logos, or false visitor numbers.
